Questions

Before you apply.

What does it cost?

Each paid step has a set price, and every build gets a written scope before any work starts. The Bottleneck Audit is $297, credited toward your build. Funnel Rescue is $1,500. Site + Funnel is $2,500–$3,500. The Care Plan is $197 per month. Smaller pieces run $27–$97, and the free board is $0.

What is the Bottleneck Audit, and does the fee come off a build?

I go through your funnel the way a buyer would, every step, on a real phone. You get a written findings doc: where people leave, ranked by what it's costing you. If you hire me for the fix, the Audit fee comes off the build.

How fast do you reply?

I read it myself and reply by email within 24 hours.

How do scope and payment work?

Fixed scope, in writing. Paid in full before work starts.

What do I get at handoff?

A handoff call with whoever will run it, plus four things every build ships with: you own the backups, a one-page note on what happens if I'm out, alerts when something fails silently, and one restore, tested before handoff. And you keep a copy of your list.

What if you're out, or something breaks?

None of this makes a system unbreakable. Platforms change under everyone, mine included. Each build leaves with the one-page note on what happens if I'm out: where everything lives and what to check first. If a form stops delivering or an automation errors, an alert goes to you.

Do I own my accounts and my list?

Your list stays in your tools. The accounts and keys that connect them are set up in your name and live in your accounts, never mine.

Will this bring me traffic?

It won't bring you traffic. It makes sure the people already showing up don't fall through.

What is the Care Plan?

Offered when I hand a build over, never sold up front. Monitoring, fixes when a platform changes underneath you, up to 2 hours of changes a month, and a 48-hour support window. Month to month, $197 per month.

What if a build is more than I can spend right now?

Start smaller. The course, the Launch Engine and the Promo Pack each do one piece, and the free board costs nothing. If you take the Audit's findings and fix it yourself, that's a fine outcome. You paid for the answer, not for permission to use it.

Who's going to rely on it?

A tool only you use is low stakes. When it breaks, you're the only one it lets down.

Most of what I build isn't like that. Your customers type their names and emails into it. Sometimes someone else runs it for you. Those people are trusting you with their information, and once it's out, it's out.

So I'm careful about where that information lives. Your list stays in your tools. The accounts and keys that connect them are set up in your name and live in your accounts, never mine. That's one less copy of your customers' details sitting somewhere you don't control. Every build works this way.
