Questions

Before you sign up

Full details: Terms and conditions (section S4-B) and Refund policy.

Is it free to start?

Yes. You can start on a free account. Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly inside your account at the prices shown there, which match Systeme.io's own prices.

How do I cancel, and what happens after?

You can cancel anytime from your account. Your paid plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then your account moves to the free plan and you won't be billed again. Your pages, contacts and emails aren't deleted, but if you're over the free plan's limits, parts of the account are restricted until you trim back or upgrade.

Do you refund?

We don't refund a month or year that has already started. Your plan won't renew after you cancel.

What if a payment fails?

It's retried up to three times and your account keeps working meanwhile. If every retry fails, the paid plan is cancelled the same way.

Can I take my contacts with me?

Yes. You can export your contacts as a CSV file whenever you want. Funnels and pages can be copied to another Systeme.io account with a share link; there's no file download for them.

Who do I ask for help?

We help with getting set up and getting into your account, by email at charles@thedigitaldad.co, and we reply within 2 business days. Questions about how a Systeme.io feature works are answered by Systeme.io's help center.

Is this Systeme.io?

The Digital Dad AI Systems is not Systeme.io. White-label accounts run on Systeme.io under The Digital Dad AI Systems' reseller account, and Systeme.io's own terms of service and acceptable use rules apply alongside ours.

What if the reseller account is suspended?

Every white-label account sits under our reseller account. Misuse of the platform by any user can put the reseller account at risk, and a suspension of the reseller account would affect every account under it. To protect all users, we may suspend or close a white-label account that breaks Systeme.io's rules or these terms.

Do you earn from my plan?

When you are on a paid plan, we earn from it.
